Even though your eye cells and muscle cells have the exact same DNA book, they only "read" the specific chapters they need!

🔬 UV SECRET: Sliding Filament: Actin + Myosin + Ca²⁺ TOPIC 02 // HOW MUSCLES MOVE

How Muscles Pull (Bicep vs. Tricep)

Muscles can only pull, never push! That’s why they work in pairs. When you bend your arm, your bicep contracts while your tricep relaxes.

Arm: Relaxed (15%) Drag slider to flex 💪
Bicep: Relaxed • Tricep: Stretched
What fuels every muscle movement?

A tiny energy molecule called ATP plus calcium signals from your nerves tell the muscle fibres when to pull together.

🔬 UV SECRET: Beta cells in Pancreas secrete Insulin TOPIC 03 // HORMONES

Body’s Chemical Text Messages

Hormones are chemical messengers that keep our body balanced—like a thermostat that cools the room down when it gets too warm.

Blood Sugar: Normal 😊
How does the body bring sugar back down?

When blood sugar rises after a meal, the pancreas releases insulin, telling cells to absorb the glucose until levels are balanced again.

🔬 UV SECRET: Hebb's Law: LTP strengthens synapses TOPIC 04 // THE BRAIN

How Practice Rewires the Brain

Whenever you practice a new song or solve physics problems, the connection between your brain cells literally gets stronger!

Connection: New Topic (Level 1)
What is this brain superpower called?

It’s called neuroplasticity — "neurons that fire together, wire together!"

🔬 UV SECRET: Snell's Law: n₁ sin(θ₁) = n₂ sin(θ₂) TOPIC 05 // PHYSICS & OPTICS

Light & Prism Rainbow Splitter

When white light enters a glass prism, each color bends at a slightly different angle because of its wavelength—splitting into VIBGYOR!

Incident Beam Angle: 45° Drag to bend light 🌈
Why does violet bend more than red light?

Violet light has a shorter wavelength and slows down slightly more inside glass than red light, causing a sharper bend (dispersion)!

What causes the tall spike on an ECG?

That sharp spike is the QRS complex — the electrical impulse telling the heart’s ventricles to contract and pump blood to your body!
