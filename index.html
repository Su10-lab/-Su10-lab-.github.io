DNA Base-Pairing & Heredity

How does a microscopic molecule store the blueprint for an entire organism? In DNA, Adenine (A) pairs with Thymine (T), and Cytosine (C) pairs with Guanine (G). Complete the complementary strand below:

Match base #1: What pairs with A?
Why do cells with identical DNA perform different functions?

Although every somatic cell shares the same genetic code, differential gene expression ensures that each cell activates only the specific genes required for its specialized role.

🔬 UV SECRET: Sliding Filament: Actin + Myosin + Calcium Ions TOPIC 02 // BIOMECHANICS

Antagonistic Muscle Action

Skeletal muscles can only exert a pulling force, never a push, which is why they operate in antagonistic pairs. Bending the elbow requires the bicep to contract while the tricep relaxes.

Flexion: 15% (Relaxed) Adjust slider to contract 💪
Bicep: Relaxed • Tricep: Extended
What powers muscle contraction at the cellular level?

Molecules of ATP provide the energy while calcium ions released by nerve impulses enable actin and myosin filaments to slide past one another.

🔬 UV SECRET: Pancreatic Beta Cells secrete Insulin TOPIC 03 // ENDOCRINOLOGY

Hormonal Homeostasis

The endocrine system maintains internal stability through chemical signaling, functioning much like a thermostat that restores balance whenever physiological levels shift.

Blood Glucose: 90 mg/dL (Stable)
How does negative feedback regulate blood glucose?

When blood glucose rises after a meal, the pancreas secretes insulin, prompting cells to absorb glucose until normal baseline levels are restored.

🔬 UV SECRET: Hebb's Law: LTP strengthens synapses TOPIC 04 // NEUROSCIENCE

Synaptic Plasticity & Memory

Every time we practice a skill or solve a challenging problem, repeated electrical signaling physically reinforces the synaptic connections between neurons.

Synaptic Strength: Baseline (Level 1)
How does experience reshape neural pathways?

Neuroplasticity allows the nervous system to strengthen frequently used synapses, making future signal transmission faster and more efficient.

🔬 UV SECRET: Snell's Law: n₁ sin(θ₁) = n₂ sin(θ₂) TOPIC 05 // WAVE OPTICS

Optical Dispersion Through a Prism

When white light enters a glass prism, each constituent wavelength refracts at a distinct angle, separating the beam into the visible spectrum (VIBGYOR).

Angle of Incidence: 45° Adjust incident angle 🌈
Why does violet light refract more sharply than red light?

Violet light has a shorter wavelength and experiences a higher refractive index in glass than red light, resulting in greater angular deviation.
