02 // CORE CURIOSITY

Small questions. Deep fascinations.

Four corners of biology I keep coming back to. Open a card to explore the questions behind my curiosity.

01 / EXPLORE

Genetics

How does a tiny molecule hold the instructions for an entire life? I’m interested in DNA, inheritance, and the possibilities of genomic medicine.

A question to explore

What makes cells with the same DNA behave differently?Most cells share the same genetic instructions, but use different parts of them. Gene expression helps explain why a nerve cell and a muscle cell have such different jobs.

02 / EXPLORE

Hormonal Functions

The body is always in conversation with itself. I’m curious about the chemical messages that regulate growth, energy, and balance.

A question to explore

How does the body know when to turn a signal down?Many hormone systems use negative feedback: a change triggers a response that reduces that change. This helps keep internal conditions within a useful range.

03 / EXPLORE

Muscle Structure & Function

Every movement starts with a remarkable chain of events. I’m exploring how muscle fibres, nerve signals, and cellular energy work together.

A question to explore

What actually happens when a muscle contracts?Inside muscle fibres, actin and myosin slide past one another, powered by ATP. Calcium signals help control when that interaction starts and stops.

04 / EXPLORE

Brain Structure & Function

How do networks of cells give rise to memory and learning? Neural connections and the brain’s ability to adapt keep me asking more.

A question to explore

How can practice change the brain?Neuroplasticity describes the nervous system’s ability to change with experience. Learning can alter the strength and organization of connections between neurons.
